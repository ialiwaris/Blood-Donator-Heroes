WELCOME TO Blood Donator Heroes, POWERED BY SEAZLY SERVICES. WE BELIEVE IN HELPING COMMUNITIES SAVE LIVES WHILE FULLY RESPECTING YOUR PERSONAL PRIVACY. THIS PRIVACY POLICY EXPLAINS HOW OUR LOCAL-FIRST MODEL PROTECTS YOUR DATA.

OUR COMMITMENT: Blood Donator Heroes IS DESIGNED WITH A PRIVACY-PRESERVING, "LOCAL-FIRST" ARCHITECTURE. YOUR PERSONAL PREFERENCES, DONATION COUNTS, AND HERO PROFILES ARE MANAGED DIRECTLY ON YOUR DEVICE STORAGE WITHOUT MANDATORY CLOUD ACCOUNT TRACKING OR DATA HARVESTING.

1. INFORMATION COLLECTION AND USE

Blood Donator Heroes PROVIDES A DIRECT WAY TO FIND DONORS AND REQUEST EMERGENCY BLOOD ASSISTANCE.

  • NO CLOUD ACCOUNTS REQUIRED: YOU DO NOT NEED TO CREATE A CENTRALIZED CLOUD ACCOUNT OR LINK SOCIAL MEDIA CREDENTIALS TO USE THE APP.
  • LOCAL DONOR & HERO PROFILE: YOUR DISPLAY NAME, CITY, AREA, BLOOD GROUP, AND DONATION MILESTONES ARE SAVED ON YOUR LOCAL DEVICE. YOU ARE ALWAYS IN CONTROL OF WHAT DETAILS YOU ENTER.
  • LOCATION & SEARCH PREFERENCES: CITY AND AREA FILTERS USED DURING DONOR MATCHING ARE PROCESSED ON-DEVICE TO DISPLAY COMPATIBLE RESULTS. WE DO NOT TRACK OR STORE YOUR REAL-TIME CONTINUOUS BACKGROUND LOCATION ON EXTERNAL SERVERS.
  • EMERGENCY REQUESTS: REQUEST DETAILS AND STATUSES CREATED WITHIN THE APP REMAIN PRIVATELY STORED ON YOUR LOCAL DEVICE STORAGE.

2. PERMISSIONS

WE ONLY REQUEST NECESSARY SYSTEM PERMISSIONS TO ENSURE FLUID APPLICATION FUNCTIONALITY:

  • NOTIFICATIONS: USED EXCLUSIVELY TO ALERT YOU OF IMPORTANT EMERGENCY BLOOD REQUESTS OR STATUS UPDATES NEAR YOU (IF ENABLED).
  • LOCAL STORAGE: REQUIRED TO SECURELY SAVE YOUR DONATION PREFERENCES, SAVED HERO CARDS, AND LOCAL APP SETTINGS.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Blood Donator Heroes DOES NOT SELL, RENT, OR TRADE YOUR PERSONAL DATA OR HEALTH PREFERENCES TO THIRD-PARTY ADVERTISING NETWORKS OR DATA BROKERS.

4. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Blood Donator Heroes IS INTENDED FOR GENERAL AUDIENCES COMPLIANT WITH LEGAL BLOOD DONATION AGE GUIDELINES. WE DO NOT KNOWINGLY COLLECT OR HARVEST PERSONALLY IDENTIFIABLE INFORMATION FROM CHILDREN UNDER 13 YEARS OF AGE.
