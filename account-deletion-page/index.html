Request account deletion.

Leave the community on your terms. Use this page to request permanent deletion of your Blood Donator Heroes account and the personal data associated with it.

No app installation or sign-in required
This page is awaiting support contact and deletion policy configuration. Deletion requests are not available here yet.

Send a deletion request

This form prepares an email. You must send it from your email app to submit your request.

Include the country code if using a phone number.

Never include your password, one-time code, or identity documents in this form.

Prefer writing directly? Email with the subject “Account deletion request”. Include your registered email or phone number.

What happens next

  1. Send your requestPrepare the email here, review it, and press Send in your email app. You can request deletion even after uninstalling the app.
  2. Confirm account ownershipSupport may contact you through your registered contact details to confirm that the account belongs to you.
  3. Receive confirmationSupport will confirm when your account and associated data have been deleted.

Processing time
The processing timeframe has not yet been published.

Deletion is permanent. Uninstalling the app or signing out does not delete your account.

Account & associated data

Your request covers your account and the personal data linked to it, including the following where stored:

  • Account identity, donor profile, and contact details.
  • Profile photo, blood group, and saved location information.
  • Personal data associated with donation activity, blood requests, and messages.

Data retention
The operator has not yet published which data, if any, must be retained or for how long.

This webpage does not upload your form entries to a server or save them in browser storage. Your request is sent through your email provider only after you send the prepared email.
