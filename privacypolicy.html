Blood Donator Heroes

Privacy Policy

Operated by SEAZLY SERVICES · Last updated: October 25, 2026

Blood Donator Heroes helps people find donors, publish blood requests, communicate, and coordinate donations. This policy explains how we handle your information.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, we process your name, username, email, account authentication information, phone and WhatsApp numbers, profile photo, date of birth, gender, blood group, donation availability and history, certificates, and location details you provide or explicitly share. We also process blood requests, patient and hospital details, messages, submitted content, notification tokens, and technical service records. Only share another person's information with appropriate permission.

2. How we use information

We use information to manage accounts, match donors and requests, enable communication, deliver notifications, maintain donation records, provide support, and protect the service. Account and service data are stored using cloud services; some settings and session information are also stored on your device.

3. Sharing and service providers

Profile details, requests, posts, and certificates may be visible to other users according to the feature and privacy settings. Messages are shared with their recipients. Authorized administrators may access information to operate the service and handle support or reports. Information you publish or send can be copied by others.

We use Supabase for authentication, database and file storage; Google for optional sign-in; and Expo and platform notification services for push delivery. Where enabled, supported web versions use Firebase Analytics for usage events. External services such as WhatsApp handle information you choose to share under their own policies. Providers may process data outside your country. We do not sell or rent personal information. We may disclose information to comply with law or protect users and the service.

4. Permissions and controls

Notifications support alerts; location supports nearby features where available; photo and media access supports selected images and saved certificates. The current app does not request continuous background location access. You can manage permissions in device settings and available profile and contact visibility controls in the app. Denying a permission may limit its feature.

5. Retention and account deletion

We retain information to operate your account and the service. To request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data, email seazlyservices@gmail.com with the subject “Blood Donator Heroes account deletion” and your registered username, email, or phone number. You do not need to reinstall the app. We may verify ownership. Never send passwords or one-time codes.

Your deletion request covers your account, profile, uploaded images, contact details, and associated personal data in requests, donation activity, messages, and posts. Uninstalling or signing out does not delete your account. Copies independently saved outside the service may remain outside our control.

6. Security and your rights

We use HTTPS, authentication, and access controls to help protect information. No system is completely secure. You can edit supported profile fields or contact us to request access, correction, or deletion, subject to identity verification and applicable requirements.

7. Children and policy changes

The app is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information. Blood donation eligibility follows applicable age and medical requirements. We may update this policy and its date as our practices change, with additional notice where required.
